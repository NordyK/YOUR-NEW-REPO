Earth Explorer

Explore the planet

  1. Drag to spin, scroll or pinch to zoom. Satellite view goes down to single buildings, and every nearby place with a Wikipedia article appears as you zoom in.
  2. Pick layers on the left: wonders, landmarks, animals (tick several to compare ranges) and natural resources.
  3. Click anything on the globe for photos, facts and figures.
  4. Drag the timeline back to the Ice Age. Borders, coastlines, ice, lakes, forests, landmarks and wildlife change with the year.

Data: Natural Earth, historical-basemaps (A. Ourednik), Wikipedia, NOAA, NASA, NSIDC, USGS, IUCN. Imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics. Ranges and prehistoric figures are approximate.
