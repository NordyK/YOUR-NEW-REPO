🌍 Welcome to Earth Explorer

  • Drag to spin the globe, scroll / pinch to zoom. In Satellite mode you can zoom right down to individual buildings.
  • Use the menu on the left to switch layers: natural wonders, landmarks, animals, natural resources and history.
  • Animals: tick one or many species to paint where they live, see animated 3D models on the globe and open a 3D viewer with facts.
  • Resources: pick oil, gold, lithium… to see the biggest deposits and colour the top producing countries.
  • Click anything — a marker, a country, an animal range — for photos, facts and figures.
  • Time machine: drag the timeline to go back up to 22,000 years. Borders, landmarks, ice sheets, coastlines, lakes, forests, extinct animals and world statistics change with the year.
