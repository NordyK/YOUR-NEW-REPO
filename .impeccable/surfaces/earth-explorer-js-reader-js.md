# Article reader

Mode: read. Extends the established night-atlas world of Earth Explorer; no new palette or type system.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An encyclopedia entry with scholarly apparatus. One calm reading column, and a margin that ties every fact to a place and a time. It refuses the default "Wikipedia page pasted in a modal" and the blog-card layout.

OWN-WORLD: Night-atlas ink panels (#0c1220 on #05080f) with one brass accent (#f0b54a) for keys, links and the active state. Newsreader for titles and section heads; Public Sans body at 17px with 1.65 leading. 1px hairline rules; no cards, shadows or tinted boxes inside the column. Margin notes in 12.5px Public Sans, each keyed with a brass index letter (raise from rw-centre-rail-reference-setting: keyed apparatus, every rule at one device pixel).

STORY: A learner opens an item from the globe, reads the explanation, sees where and when it sits in the margin, then jumps back to the globe, the year or a related place.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim running head (kind · place · year on the left; Contents, Back to globe and Wikipedia on the right). Under it, the title and a one-paragraph standfirst, then the lead photo at full column width. The column is about 68ch. A 240px outer margin carries the locator (coordinates, Show on globe), key figures, dates as timeline jumps, related items and sources.

FORM: #5 of 7, encyclopedia entry with margin apparatus. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
